# Inbox - freshly captured candidate notes

Agents (via the post-task capture hook) and humans drop `status: candidate` notes here after a task. Curate regularly: link, tag, dedupe, move into the right section, and eval-gate behavior-changing lessons to `trusted`.

Nothing here is trusted or steers generation until curated + gated.

This map deliberately does not list the notes in this folder - a hand-maintained list would drift, which is exactly how the vault became invisible before. Most vault notes currently live here, uncurated. See [[MANIFEST]] for the complete generated catalog.

Uncurated does not mean unusable: a `candidate` note is an authoritative record of what a past session tried, what the oracle said, and what failed. It is only not a correctness claim.
